# Profile: Alternative News

Find financially relevant developments and analysis that can add information,
evidence or perspectives likely to be missed or underweighted by mainstream
financial-news coverage.

The normal market-impact hierarchy still applies.

Give additional value to:

- original reporting;
- independent analysis based on evidence;
- developments receiving limited mainstream attention despite plausible
  financial significance;
- credible contrarian interpretations;
- emerging risks;
- second-order effects;
- structural economic or geopolitical developments.

Do not reward a story merely because it is contrarian, anti-consensus or from
an alternative source.

Penalize:

- unsupported speculation;
- ideological commentary without new information;
- sensationalism;
- conspiracy claims without credible evidence;
- recycled mainstream stories presented as alternative insight.

A well-supported overlooked Tier 4 story should rank highly.

An unsupported dramatic claim should rank low regardless of how unusual it is.

## Selection discipline

The Alternative profile changes ranking emphasis more than eligibility.
Alternative or overlooked value should raise a materially relevant story in the
ranking, but a story does not need to prove that mainstream outlets ignored it
in order to remain selected. The source set already provides much of the
alternative-news character.

For `selected: true`, require a credible headline-supported reason that the
story could matter to investment decisions, macroeconomics, a significant
sector, policy, funding, commodities, rates, FX, risk or an important business.
Differentiated evidence, second-order effects, emerging risks and credible
contrarian interpretations should increase rank and priority.

Do not exclude a materially useful story merely because its alternative angle
is modest. Likewise, novelty, ideology or contrarian framing without financial
materiality is not enough. Local or narrow developments may still be selected
when their direct financial significance is material; they should simply rank
below broader or more consequential developments.

When uncertain, prefer retaining a credible materially relevant story and let
the ranking express that it is lower priority.
