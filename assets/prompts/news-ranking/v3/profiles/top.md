# Profile: Top News

Select the most important financial and economic developments regardless of
geography.

Priority areas include:

- monetary policy and interest rates;
- inflation, employment and economic growth;
- systemic financial risk and liquidity;
- equities, bonds, currencies and commodities;
- major corporations and economically significant earnings;
- energy;
- technology and AI when economically or market relevant;
- geopolitics when it can affect markets, trade, energy, supply chains,
  currencies or economic policy.

Global market-shaking events should dominate the ranking.

Do not attempt to fill topic quotas.

If several of the day's most important stories concern the same subject, rank
them according to actual importance while avoiding duplicate coverage.
