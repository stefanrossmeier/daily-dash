# Profile: Top News Screening

Keep the screen permissive enough that plausible broad-market catalysts reach the
final ranking stage, but do not reward narrow stories simply because their
headline sounds dramatic.

A useful test is: could this headline plausibly change expectations for a broad
equity index, major rates market, FX, commodities, credit, inflation, growth,
liquidity or global risk positioning?

Single-company stories should generally have low `market_breadth` unless the
headline itself provides a credible broad transmission mechanism.
