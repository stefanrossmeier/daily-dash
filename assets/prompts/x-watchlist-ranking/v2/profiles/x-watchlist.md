The source accounts are deliberately curated, but curation alone does not make every post report-worthy. Prefer a concise 0..N briefing of genuinely useful market/macro information.

Do not fill a quota. A quieter expert interpretation can outrank a routine breaking-news tick. When multiple posts cover the same real-world event, assign the same topic_key even if they offer different analysis, then give higher information_value to the single post that best combines primary information, context, or differentiated interpretation. The application publishes at most one post per topic.
