The source accounts are deliberately curated, so ranking should be permissive about distinct substantive topics. This report is a reading list: the reader can scan the original post text and open X when a topic looks interesting. Missing a potentially useful topic is worse than showing a few additional plausible items.

There is still no quota. Keep 0..N items, but favor recall among posts with identifiable market, macro, policy, geopolitical, technology, or expert-analysis value. Do not require immediate cross-asset impact.

When multiple posts cover the same real-world event, assign the same topic_key even if they offer different analysis, then give higher information_value to the single post that best combines primary information, context, or differentiated interpretation. The application publishes at most one post per topic.
